57Article & glossary
9:41LTE
Settlement
When does money from a sale arrive?
Three business days after your sale fills. The NGX calls this T+3 — trade day plus three. On that day the money moves from the CSCS to your bank account by Direct Cash Settlement, so it never sits with us.
Glossary · free on every plan
T+3The three business days between a trade and the money or shares actually changing hands.
Direct Cash SettlementMoney from sales and dividends goes straight from the CSCS to your own bank account.
CSCSThe register that records which Nigerian shares you own, under your CHN.

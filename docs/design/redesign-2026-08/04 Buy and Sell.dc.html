Buy and Sell
How to buy, then price, then shares, then review, then PIN. Buying now skips the price step. The review states the order type, your price, the commission and the rules that bite: no cancelling once the market opens, unfilled limit orders expire at 4:30pm. Sell mirrors it and asks one extra question, where the money should go.
